Kubrick

A private studio tool

Kubrick is the tool its owner uses to make short videos and publish them on their own channels: YouTube, Instagram and Facebook.

With the owner's permission it uploads the videos they choose to their own YouTube channels, and reads the numbers of those videos (views, watch time, comments), so the owner can see how each video is doing.

Kubrick runs on the owner's computer and is used only by its owner, with their own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.
