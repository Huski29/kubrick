Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Kubrick is a private tool. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own accounts.

What Kubrick uses

When the owner connects one of their YouTube channels, Kubrick uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload the videos the owner chooses to that channel, with their titles and descriptions;
  • read the numbers of those videos: views, watch time, retention, likes and comments.

Where it stays

The access keys and the numbers are stored only on the owner's computer. Kubrick does not send them to anyone, does not sell them, does not use them for advertising and does not use them to train AI models.

Kubrick's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

Kubrick uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account permissions page. Deleting Kubrick's folder on the computer deletes everything it stored.
