Data deletion

Last updated 16 November 2026

Kubrick is a private tool used only by its owner. Everything it stores stays on the owner's computer: the access keys, the videos it published and their numbers.

How to delete your data

  • Remove Kubrick from Facebook's Business Integrations settings (for Facebook and Instagram) or from the Google account permissions page (for YouTube). From that moment Kubrick can no longer read or publish anything.
  • Delete Kubrick's folder on the computer: everything it stored is gone.
  • If you are not the owner and think Kubrick holds something about you, open an issue on this site's page on GitHub and it will be deleted within 30 days.
